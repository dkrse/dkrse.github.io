Preprints

  • A negative result on a putative √ω(g) correction to Wolf's consecutive-prime-gap formula, with a reproduction of finite-size drift to 3·1014.
    Sestak, K. (2026). Zenodo. doi:10.5281/zenodo.20010072
    Current version. The apparent √ω(g) residual reported in v1–v3 below is shown to be an artifact of a mis-transcribed baseline; against the correct Wolf 2014 reference the residual collapses by 78–95% and the out-of-sample weighted R2 on N > 1013 is −18.3. Wolf's finite-size drift in a(x), s(x) is reproduced on the extended range.
    Code & data: github.com/dkrse/math-final-wolf
